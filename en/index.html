FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to the questions you're asking before starting a project.

What are your typical production lead times?

+

Most projects are fabricated within two to four weeks. For complex structures or larger series, we set out a precise schedule as soon as the quote is confirmed.

How do you handle transport and installation of large structures?

+

We design every piece to be easily dismantled and transported. We handle the logistics and can install on site with our own teams or in coordination with yours.

What's the environmental impact of your materials, and how are they processed?

+

Our offcuts and EPS foam are 100% recycled through a local circular network. The lightness of our structures also reduces transport's carbon footprint -- a concrete advantage for your events.

Can you build reusable elements for multiple events?

+

Yes. We design modular, demountable structures built to be reused, and advise you on the materials and finishes best suited to repeated use.

Do you work with event agencies?

+

Of course. We regularly collaborate with agencies, scenographers and visual merchandising managers, and can work as a subcontractor or directly with you, depending on your needs.
